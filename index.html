Privacy Policy for ResuMateAI Bot

Last Updated: May 1, 2026

ResuMateAI ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle the information you provide when using the ResuMateAI Telegram Bot.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with ResuMateAI, we may collect the following:

  • Basic Telegram Data: Your Telegram User ID, username, and language preference to facilitate communication.
  • User Inputs: Job descriptions (JDs) or prompt texts you send to the bot.
  • Resume Files: PDF files or other documents you upload for analysis.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly to provide the core service of the bot:

  • To analyze your resume against specific job descriptions.
  • To generate ATS-focused formatting and content improvement suggestions.
  • To return the analysis results to your Telegram chat.

3. Third-Party AI Services

To generate high-quality analysis, the text extracted from your resume and job descriptions is securely transmitted to third-party Large Language Model (LLM) API providers (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.) for processing. We do not permit these third-party providers to use your personal data to train their fundamental AI models.

4. Data Storage and Retention

  • No Permanent Storage: We do not permanently store your resume files on our servers. Files are processed in memory or temporarily stored during the analysis phase and are deleted immediately after the response is generated.
  • Chat History: Your chat history is governed by Telegram’s platform policies. You can delete the chat on your end at any time to clear the conversation.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or resume data to any third parties, recruiters, or marketing agencies.

6. Your Rights

You have full control over your data. You can delete your interaction history directly within the Telegram app. If you have any privacy-related concerns, please contact us.
